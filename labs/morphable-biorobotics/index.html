Overview

The laboratory investigates how robotics can create new capabilities in medicine, surgery, rehabilitation, and exploration.

My primary work centered on minimally invasive soft robotic devices. I also received a Boston University UROP Student Research Award in Fall 2022 for work on the laparoscopy project.
