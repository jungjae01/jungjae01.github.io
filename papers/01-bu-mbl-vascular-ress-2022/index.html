Minimally Invasive Soft Robotic Prototypes Provide Variable Occlusion in a Simplified Aortic Flow Model

L. Kinnicutt, A. Pathak, T. Bohac, A. Peng, C. Taglietti, C. Lee, R. Rauf, Jungjae Lee, LZ. Heng, J. Rogatinsky, K. McDonald, J. Siracuse, and T. Ranzani

Morphable Biorobotics Laboratory, Boston University

American Heart Association Resuscitation Science Symposium, Chicago, USA, 2022. Presented.

  • Poster
  • Source project page

TL;DR

A poster presentation on minimally invasive soft robotic prototypes for variable occlusion in a simplified aortic flow model.

Overview

The prior portfolio connects this presentation to a cardiovascular project involving an in-vitro setup and a PCB for controlling valves and pumps.

Key Contributions

  • Assisted with assembly of an in-vitro cardiovascular setup.
  • Designed an EagleCAD PCB to control valves and pumps for the testing platform.
