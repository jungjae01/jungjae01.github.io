Jungjae Lee

I am a Computer Engineering student at Boston University with a double concentration in robotics and machine learning, and a minor in innovation and entrepreneurship. My work spans robotic perception, planning, simulation, soft robotic systems, and applied machine learning for autonomous and real-world robotic platforms.

I have contributed to research at Seoul National University and Boston University, including autonomous robot intelligence, soft robot simulation, medical soft robotics, and computational biomedicine projects. I am especially interested in systems that connect simulation, planning, control, and physical robot deployment.
